# Demoz: 6-Month Product Roadmap & Expansion Strategy

## 1. Executive Summary
Demoz aims to become the definitive workforce management solution for the Ethiopian market by bridging the gap between attendance tracking, local regulatory compliance, and digital payment infrastructure. This roadmap outlines our transition from an MVP to a comprehensive enterprise resource planning (ERP) lite system, coupled with a strategic expansion into Ethiopia's growing regional economic hubs.

---

## 2. Product Roadmap (6-Month Phased Plan)

### Phase 1: Market Entry & Core Payouts (Months 1-1.5) - *Current State*
*   **Focus:** Stabilizing the core "Trifecta" (Attendance → Compliance → Payment).
*   **Key Features:**
    *   GPS/Geofencing attendance via mobile app.
    *   Automated Schedule A Tax and POESSA Pension calculation.
    *   Bulk salary disbursements via Chapa (Telebirr, CBE Birr, Bank).
    *   Basic regulatory report exports (SIGTAS-ready).
*   **Milestone:** Successful pilot with 5-10 Addis-based SMEs.

### Phase 2: Multi-Entity Support & Advanced HR (Months 1.5-3)
*   **Focus:** Scaling the system for larger holding companies and complex organizations.
*   **Key Features:**
    *   **Multi-Entity Management:** Single login for admins managing multiple TINs/Organizations.
    *   **Advanced Leave Management:** Custom leave policies (maternity, bereavement, study leave) with automated balance tracking.
    *   **Employee Self-Service (ESS) Portal:** Enhanced mobile features for document uploads (ID cards, degree certificates).
    *   **Approval Workflows:** Multi-level approval chains for leave and overtime.

### Phase 3: Financial Ecosystem Integration (Months 3-4.5)
*   **Focus:** Seamless data flow between HR and Finance departments.
*   **Key Features:**
    *   **Accounting Integrations:** One-click export/sync with **Peachtree (Sage 50)** and **QuickBooks**, which are widely used by Ethiopian accountants.
    *   **Direct Bank APIs:** Integration with major commercial bank corporate APIs for real-time reconciliation.
    *   **Performance-Linked Bonuses:** Tiered incentive calculations based on attendance and custom KPIs.

### Phase 4: AI-Driven Analytics & Optimization (Months 4.5-6)
*   **Focus:** Using data to drive operational efficiency.
*   **Key Features:**
    *   **Smart Shift Optimization:** AI suggestions for shift scheduling based on historical peak attendance and labor costs.
    *   **Predictive Attrition Modeling:** Identifying flight risks based on attendance patterns and leave behavior.
    *   **Voice-Activated Attendance:** Pilot for voice-biometric check-in for hands-free environments (e.g., manufacturing).

## 3. Local Market Expansion Strategy

### 3.1 Geographic Expansion
While Addis Ababa is the primary market, significant growth is happening in regional industrial zones.
*   **Target City 1: Dire Dawa (Month 3):** Focus on the Free Trade Zone and logistics companies.
*   **Target City 2: Bahir Dar & Hawassa (Month 4):** Focus on the textile and manufacturing sectors within the Industrial Parks.
*   **Target City 3: Mekelle & Adama (Month 5):** Re-engaging with the agro-processing and manufacturing hubs.

### 3.2 Sector-Specific Strategy
*   **Manufacturing/Industrial Parks:** High density of hourly workers makes the GPS attendance and automated overtime calculation a high-value proposition.
*   **SME Services (Addis):** High demand for "Telebirr-first" payroll systems to reduce the administrative burden of bank runs.
*   **Remote/NGO Sector:** Focus on geofencing for field staff and multi-currency reporting capabilities.

## 4. Key Success Metrics (KPIs)
*   **User Growth:** Reach 50,000 active employees on the platform by Month 6.
*   **Payment Volume:** Process 500M ETB in monthly bulk disbursements.
*   **Retention:** Maintain a 95% month-over-month retention rate for HR Managers.
*   **Compliance Accuracy:** Zero reported errors in tax/pension filings generated by the system.
