## 1. Unique Value Propositions (UVPs)

### 1.1 Automated Local Compliance
Forget Excel formulas. Demoz has the **Ethiopian Compliance Engine** built-in. We handle the 7% Pension (POESSA) and the progressive Schedule A Income Tax brackets (0-35%) automatically, ensuring you never miscalculate a staff salary again.

### 1.2 Instant Bulk Payouts via Telebirr & CBE Birr
Stop writing manual bank letters. With our **Chapa integration**, you can pay your entire team’s salaries directly to their **Telebirr**, **CBE Birr**, or commercial bank accounts with a single click.

### 1.3 Native Ethiopic Calendar & Language Support
Work in the calendar your team uses. Demoz features native support for the **Ethiopic (Ge'ez) calendar** and a full **Amharic UI**, making it intuitive for every employee, from the field to the office.

### 1.4 Labor Law Guardrails
Stay on the right side of the law. The system automatically enforces the **Ethiopian Labor Proclamation No. 1156/2019**, flagging overtime violations (beyond 2h/day or 20h/month) and applying the correct **2.5x holiday multiplier** for 2026.

### 1.5 Offline-First GPS Attendance
Ethiopia’s internet can be spotty, but your attendance tracking shouldn't be. Our mobile app allows employees to check in via **GPS/Geofencing** offline. Logs sync automatically as soon as they are back in coverage.
